Who can be the donor

  • Must be 18 to 65 years old
  • Must weigh at least 50 kg
  • Should be in good general health
  • Hemoglobin level should be normal
  • Body temperature and blood pressure should be within safe limits
  • Must not have donated blood in the last 3 months

Why to donate blood

Why should you donate blood?

  1. Blood donation save lifes.One unit can save upto three people.
  2. Blood cannot be manufactured-it only comes from generous donors like you
  3. Donating helps maintain an adequate supply for emergencies,surgeries,and cancer treatment
  4. It's good for your health! Regular blood donation can reduce iron levels,keeping heart healthy
