Who can be the donor

  • Must be 18 to 65 years old
  • Must weigh at least 50 kg
  • Should be in good general health
  • Hemoglobin level should be normal
  • Body temperature and blood pressure should be within safe limits
  • Must not have donated blood in the last 3 months
